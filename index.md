# Waystep Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

Waystep is a calorie and health tracker for Android, made by Wanderer Studios (a one-person
developer). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and what you can do
about it.

**The short version:** your data stays on your phone. Waystep has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share your data. The only things that leave your
phone are food searches, barcode lookups, and feedback you choose to send.

## 1. Data stored on your phone

Waystep keeps this data in the app's private storage on your device:

- **Profile:** age, sex, height, weight, activity level, metric or imperial units.
- **Goals:** calorie, macro and activity goals, and each day's targets.
- **Food log:** foods and meals you log, amounts, and your saved meals and recipes.
- **Weight entries** you add in the app.
- **Health summaries:** daily values read from Health Connect (see below), kept so the app
  opens quickly.
- **Settings:** your preferences, Home layout and reminder time.

We (the developer) can't see any of it. There is no Waystep server that stores it.

## 2. Health Connect

With your permission, Waystep reads these from Health Connect: steps, distance, floors climbed,
calories burned (total and active), exercise sessions, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate,
heart rate variability, blood oxygen, VO2 max and weight. It can also read data from before you
first connected, so your history and trends are complete.

Waystep **writes** one type of data to Health Connect: **weight**, when you log your weight in
the app.

If you allow it, Waystep also reads Health Connect in the background (about every 15 minutes) so
the Home screen is up to date when you open it. This is optional.

How we use Health Connect data:

- Only to show your health and activity in the app and to work out your calorie and macro
  goals.
- It is **never sent off your phone**, never shared with or sold to anyone, and never used for
  ads or credit or insurance decisions.
- Waystep's use of data from Health Connect follows the
  [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
  including its Limited Use requirements.

You can change or remove these permissions at any time in the Health Connect app or in your
phone's settings.

## 3. Data that leaves your phone

| What | Sent to | When | What is sent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food search | [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy) | When you search for a product | The words you search for and your country (from your SIM or language settings, to show local products) |
| Barcode lookup | Open Food Facts | When you scan a barcode | The barcode number |
| Feedback (test versions only) | The developer, by email (via [Twilio SendGrid](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy)) | Only when you tap Send | Your message, phone model, Android version, app version, time, and a screenshot of the app screen if you leave it attached |

Open Food Facts is a free, open food database. Like any website, it receives your IP address with
each request. Waystep doesn't send any personal or health data with searches.

Feedback screenshots show whatever is on screen, which may include your own numbers. You can
remove the screenshot before sending. Feedback emails are used only to fix bugs and improve the
app, and are deleted once they are no longer needed.

## 4. Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes. Scanning happens on your phone using Google's ML Kit;
camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google basic, anonymous information
about how the scanner performs (for example, speed and errors). See
[ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

## 5. Notifications

Daily check-in reminders are created on your phone. No data is sent to show them.

## 6. Backups and Google Play

- **Android backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include
  Waystep's data in your device backup to your Google account. This is handled by Google under
  your Google account settings; we can't access it.
- **Google Play:** Google Play may share crash reports and app performance data with us if you
  allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics. These don't contain your Waystep data.

## 7. What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising, no ad IDs, no analytics or tracking tools.
- No selling, renting or sharing of your data.
- No location tracking.

## 8. Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it:

- **You → Settings → Your data → Clear all food data** deletes your logged food.
- **Uninstalling Waystep** (or clearing its storage in Android settings) deletes everything it
  stored on your phone.
- Data Waystep wrote to Health Connect (weight) stays there until you delete it in Health Connect.
- To have a feedback email deleted, write to us (see Contact).

## 9. Children

Waystep is not meant for children under 16 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have
the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to its use.
Because almost everything is stored only on your phone, you control it directly in the app. For
anything else, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Big changes will
also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
